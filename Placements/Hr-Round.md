
# Hr Round

HR round questions with situation-based answers, explained in simple point-wise format:

### 1. You are leading a team, but 2 members are not cooperating. What will you do?
✅ Answer:
Step 1: Talk to them privately to understand the issue.

Step 2: Clarify the importance of their role in the project.

Step 3: Try to motivate them by assigning tasks matching their strengths.

Step 4: If issue continues, escalate it politely to the higher authority.

Goal: Keep team harmony and ensure project success.

### 2. What would you do if your manager gives you unrealistic deadlines?
✅ Answer:

Step 1: Politely explain the time needed based on workload.

Step 2: Provide a clear timeline with justified reasoning.

Step 3: Offer possible compromises like phased delivery.

Step 4: If still required, put extra effort to complete without compromising quality.


 ###  3. What if you made a mistake during a project?
✅ Answer:

Step 1: Immediately accept the mistake without hiding it.

Step 2: Inform the team/mentor and take responsibility.

Step 3: Work on fixing it quickly and learn from it.

Step 4: Implement checks so it doesn’t repeat again.

 ### 5. How do you handle stress or pressure?
✅ Answer:

Break big tasks into smaller goals.

Use to-do lists and prioritization techniques.

Take short breaks and keep a calm mind.

Focus on solutions, not the problem.

 ### 6. Tell me about a time you showed leadership.

In SIH Hackathon, I led my team to develop a real-time solution.

We were finalists — proof of good leadership & teamwork.

 ### 7. What if you get negative feedback from your manager?

### 11. You have multiple tasks and less time. How will you manage?
